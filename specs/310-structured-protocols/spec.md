# Feature Specification: Protocols as data

**Feature Branch**: `310-structured-protocols`
**Created**: 2026-10-23
**Status**: Implemented (pull requests for issue #310)
**Input**: Issue #310 and the mobile audit attached to it -- an uploaded HTML protocol is a
15-column table in a frame, unreadable on a phone and blind to the site's skins. The maintainer
decided:

- the timing software sends the results as data and the site draws them, for a phone and a desktop
  and in every skin; local HTML and its FTP upload stay as they are;
- the data logic lives in the generator: new columns, header and footer lines and kinds of tables
  (rounds, heats) need a change in the generator only; the look belongs to the site;
- existing HTML protocols are never lost: they stay as they are, and a protocol re-generated by the
  new software is shown in the new way; the last upload wins;
- links in protocols (to Strava activities and elsewhere) stay;
- the priority modes are Mass/Splitted, Custom Start and Number of Tries.

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - A rider finds their result on a phone (Priority: P1)

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a protocol uploaded as a document, **When** it opens on a 320-390 px screen, **Then**
   place, number, name and result show, the page does not scroll sideways, and a row's button opens
   the rest of its columns.
2. **Given** the same page, **Then** search by name or number, the group filter and the document's
   column switches work, and the reasons for a DSQ are visible under the row.

### User Story 2 - The referee changes the protocol without the site (Priority: P1)

1. **Given** the generator adds a column, a header line or a section of heats, **Then** the site
   draws it without a release: the document describes itself (schema 1).

### User Story 3 - Live results stay true (Priority: P1)

1. **Given** a live protocol, **Then** the page swaps in a newer upload only once its content
   arrived, and ends live only on the content on screen (see specs/site/protocols.md).

### User Story 4 - Nothing is lost (Priority: P1)

1. **Given** an HTML protocol already on the site, **Then** it shows as before.
2. **Given** an upload of HTML and a document together, **Then** the document is shown and the HTML
   linked as the original; a document the site cannot accept falls back to the HTML.

### Edge Cases

- A document of an unknown major schema, or one too big to draw (cell budget): refused, the HTML of
  the same upload kept.
- An upload that fails half-way leaves the protocol as it was.

## Requirements

FR-PRO-005 to FR-PRO-010 in `specs/site/protocols.md`.

## Success Criteria

- The audit's protocol findings are closed for protocols uploaded as documents.
- The generators' HTML stays byte for byte what it was (their golden tests).
