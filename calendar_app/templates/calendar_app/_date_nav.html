{% load i18n %} {% comment %}The arrows, Today, the month and the year above the calendar, the list and the map (issue #330): one row, the same on all three tabs, so switching tabs moves nothing about. On the list and the map it drives the page's two date fields (from_selector, to_selector) through window.setDateFields from _quick_dates.html, included before it, and each page answers the change as it already does: the list reloads, the map redraws. The arrows keep the shape on screen -- a weekend moves a week, a season a year, anything else a calendar month. Today goes back to the page's own default: on the map the thirty days ahead; on the list (today_clears) the fields are emptied and the server picks, since a list about a city or a discipline defaults to the whole season ahead and thirty days would cut that page short. The calendar passes no selectors and answers through window.onDateNav(action) itself, where action is {step: -1 or 1}, {today: true} or {year, month}; it keeps the pickers in step with what it shows through window.syncDateNav(date).{% endcomment %}
