{% load i18n %} {% comment %}The period on screen, said in words, and the way about it -- one row, the same above the calendar, the list and the map (issue #330), so switching tabs moves nothing and keeps the period. Four kinds: today and tomorrow, the weekend, the week (Monday to Sunday) and the calendar month; a year is reached by tapping the year in the label. The label always says what is shown ("October 2026", "24-25 October 2026", "2026, whole year"), the arrows step by the kind on screen, and Today goes to the same kind around today. Tapping the month opens the twelve months with their own year arrows: paging the years there changes nothing until a month is picked, so a reader looking for next March does not drag the calendar through every month on the way. The current month starts at today, as the calendar's own list does: the days already gone are not what anybody opens it for. Each page answers a change through window.onPeriodChange({kind, from, to}) -- the list and the map fill their date fields (from_selector, to_selector) and reload or redraw, the calendar changes its view -- and tells the row what it shows through window.periodNav.show(from, to). The list and the map count from the site's own day (server_today), the day their defaults are counted from; the calendar from the browser's, as FullCalendar itself does.{% endcomment %}
